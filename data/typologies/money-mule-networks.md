## How mule networks move value

A money mule receives funds and forwards them at the direction of another
party, usually retaining a fraction. Europol work on the pattern describes
recruitment at scale, with each participant handling modest amounts so that no
individual account draws attention.

The laundering value of the arrangement is dispersion. Value that would be
conspicuous moving directly instead moves through many parties with no apparent
relationship to one another or to the original source.

## Structural signature

Mule networks produce a recognisable shape: a wide layer of intermediaries,
each with brief holding periods and little activity beyond receiving and
forwarding, sitting between a small number of sources and a small number of
ultimate destinations. Individual participants look unremarkable; the
arrangement is visible only when the layer is considered as a whole.

The corollary is that mule activity is difficult to establish from a single
account or address in isolation. Evidence generally rests on the position an
account occupies relative to others, which is why analysis of these networks
depends on relationships rather than on individual transaction attributes.
